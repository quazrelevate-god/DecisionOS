User Manual.

Everything you need to run your company on DecisionOS — from capturing a spoken decision to tracking it through to done. This guide covers both the web and mobile app, with clearly marked sections for Owners and Team Members.

For Owners For Team Members For Everyone

1 · Welcome to DecisionOS

DecisionOS turns the way you already work — talking, messaging, forwarding documents — into a structured, shared operating system for your business. You speak or type a decision, and the AI turns it into tasks assigned to the right people, tracks progress, and keeps a company-wide memory.

What DecisionOS does for you

  • Captures decisions — by voice, text, or WhatsApp — and structures them into tasks automatically.
  • Assigns work to the right person or department and guides them step-by-step.
  • Keeps a Company Brain — every customer, supplier, invoice, and decision, searchable and ask-able in plain English.
  • Gives the owner a daily brief of what needs attention — approvals, overdue work, and “fires to put out”.
  • Handles routine ops — leave requests, document filing, meeting notes — so nothing slips.
New here?  Read sections 2–4 first. Then jump to the section for your role. Owners should also read 11–17.

2 · Getting Started — Signing In

For Everyone

Open your workspace link in any browser (works on phone too). You can sign in two ways:

Option A — Email & Password

  1. Make sure the Email & Password tab is selected.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Tap Sign In.

Option B — Mobile OTP (no password)

  1. Tap the Mobile OTP tab.
  2. Enter your registered mobile number and tap to send the code.
  3. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS and verify. You’re in.
Invited by your owner?  Open the invite link they sent you — it pre-fills your details and signs you in with a one-time code. No password needed.
Dark mode  Use the sun/moon icon (top-right) to switch between light and dark themes anytime.

For Everyone

5 · Decision Desk — Capturing a Decision

For OwnersTeam (if enabled)

This is the heart of DecisionOS. Speak or type what you’ve decided, and the AI structures it into tasks for the right people.

Speak a decision

  1. Pick a language chip: Auto, EN, தமிழ், or Tanglish (casual Tamil-English).
  2. Tap the microphone and speak naturally — e.g. “Tell Priya to follow up with the Bangalore retailer today.”
  3. Tap again to stop. The AI transcribes and structures it — you’ll see a short “structuring in process” note.
  4. When it’s ready, an Execution Summary shows what was created — tasks, people assigned, meetings, reminders.

Type a decision

  1. Type your instruction in the Type a Directive box.
  2. Tap Structure It.
  3. If the instruction is vague, DecisionOS may ask a couple of quick clarifying questions first — answer or skip.
One person = one task.  If a decision is for one person, DecisionOS creates a single task for them (the step-by-step breakdown lives inside that task’s AI Execution Guide). If it involves several people, each gets their own task.
Filter the feed  Use the chips (Customer, Supplier, Invoice, Payment, Complaint, Task, Approval, Reminder) to focus your Tasks & Activity list.

6 · Approving Decisions & Assigning Work

For OwnersTeam with “Approve Decisions”

Decisions captured from a directive start as Pending Approval — their tasks stay blocked until approved. This gives you a checkpoint before work goes live.

  1. On the Decision Desk, find the decision card marked Pending your approval.
  2. Review the auto-created tasks and who they’re assigned to.
  3. Need to add someone? Use Add team member / task to attach another task.
  4. Tap Approve to release the work (tasks unblock and appear in each person’s My Work), or Reject to cancel everything it created.
Delegate approvals.  Owners can grant the “Approve Decisions” access to a trusted team member so they can approve and assign without you.

7 · My Work & Tasks

For Everyone

My Work is your personal to-do list. Tasks are grouped by type, and each card shows priority, due date, progress and the latest update.

  1. Use the tabs to filter by category, or Completed to review finished work.
  2. Set a task’s Status and Progress from the dropdowns on the card.
  3. Tap Complete when done (you’ll be asked to confirm). Completed by mistake? Use Reopen.
  4. Turn on AI Priority to re-order tasks by impact, revenue, risk and urgency.
Owners: toggle My Tasks / All Tasks to switch between your own list and every task in the company (with assignee names).

Hand off or escalate

Open a task’s Activity & Handoffs to log a note, hand off to a colleague, or escalate to the owner. The original task stays and a linked follow-up is created and notified.

8 · AI Execution Guide

For Everyone

Not sure how to do a task? Let the AI break it into a checklist and coach you through each step.

  1. On a task, tap Generate AI Plan. The AI drafts a step-by-step checklist tailored to the task.
  2. Edit if needed — add, remove or reorder steps — then Accept & start.
  3. Tick steps off as you go; the task’s progress bar updates automatically (and moves to Done at 100%).
  4. Stuck on a step? Tap Ask AI on that step for a suggestion, script, or how to handle an objection.
Why single tasks?  Because the guide already breaks the work into steps, DecisionOS keeps one person’s job as a single task instead of many confusing cards.

9 · Proof of Work (Photos & Voice)

For Everyone

Attach evidence to a task — a photo of completed work or a quick voice note.

  1. On an active task, tap Photo to snap/upload an image, or Voice Reply to record.
  2. The attachments appear on the task as a “Proof of work” block.
  3. Anyone viewing the task — the assignee and the owner (in All Tasks) — can click a photo to view it full-size and play the voice note inline.
Owners: proof also shows up in the CEO Brief completed drill-down, so you can review work at a glance.

10 · Leave & Absence

For Everyone

Request time off and get it approved — all inside My Work.

For Team Members

  1. Go to My Work and open the Leave view.
  2. Tap Request Leave, pick dates & reason, and submit. Your approver is notified.
  3. Out sick today? Use Emergency Absence to flag it immediately.

For Owners / Approvers

  1. Open the Approvals tab in Leave to see pending requests.
  2. Approve, Reject, or Request info. Approved leave shows on the calendar and in staffing counts.
  3. Owners can map who approves whom under the Leave Settings (or per-member Reporting Manager).

11 · CEO Brief

For OwnersPersonal brief for team

Your daily command centre. Owners see a company-wide brief with counters and a red “Fires to put out today” banner; team members see a personal version of their own work.

  1. Switch periods (Morning / Evening / Weekly / Monthly) to change the view.
  2. Click any counter to open its list and act right there — approve decisions, review purchases, resolve complaints.
  3. Use Send Daily Digest (top bar) to email yourself the summary.

12 · People — Team, Customers & Suppliers

Team tab: OwnersContacts: as enabled

Add & manage team members (Owners)

  1. On the Employees tab, tap Add Member. Enter name, role, mobile/email.
  2. Choose Password Login or Mobile OTP (passwordless) sign-in.
  3. Tap Access on any member to grant/revoke modules (Inbox, People, Finance, Company Brain, Approve Decisions, Approve Leave, etc.).
  4. Use Invite to generate a one-tap link (share via WhatsApp).

Customers & Suppliers

Add contacts with company, phone, GST, tags and notes. Click any contact for a 360° profile — bills, payments, outstanding balance, follow-ups, complaints and relationship intelligence.

13 · Company Brain

As enabled

Ask your business anything in plain English, or search your records directly.

  1. On the Ask tab, type a question — e.g. “Who owes us the most?”, “What did we decide about the Delhi order?”
  2. Read the AI answer with links to the source records.
  3. Switch to Search to look up a specific customer, invoice or decision.
Money answers (outstanding, payments) are only shown to Owners and Finance access.

14 · Capture — Documents & WhatsApp

For OwnersTeam with Capture access

Get invoices, bills and instructions into DecisionOS — by uploading a file or forwarding on WhatsApp.

Upload a document

  1. Tap PDF / Photo (invoice, bill, receipt) or CSV / Excel (contact/sales lists).
  2. The AI reads it and shows an editable review panel — check the type, party and amounts.
  3. Tap View to open the original attachment, then File it to save the records.

WhatsApp Smart Capture

  1. Scan the QR (or save the number) and forward an invoice, photo or a quick instruction on WhatsApp.
  2. DecisionOS reads it and creates a Capture Draft — nothing auto-executes.
  3. It’s routed to the right department’s Review Queue (see section 15) for approval.
Smart routing.  A message like “prepare an estimate” goes to the relevant department (e.g. Sales/Finance), not the owner — unless it’s high-value or a policy/approval item.

15 · Review Queue

Owners see allDept. members see theirs
  1. Open Capture → Review Queue. Filter by Pending / Clarification / Approved / Rejected.
  2. Review each card’s AI classification, amount and any escalation warning.
  3. Approve (files/creates the work & assigns it), Edit, Reassign, Clarify (asks the sender back on WhatsApp), or Reject.
High-value & policy items are escalated to the Owner for sign-off automatically, based on your threshold in Settings.

16 · Meeting Notes

As enabled
  1. Tap the mic to record the meeting, or paste a transcript.
  2. DecisionOS produces a title, summary, key points, decisions and action items.
  3. Action items are auto-created as tasks and assigned to the named people.

17 · Settings (Owner)

For Owners

Configure your workspace — company profile and the rules that drive routing & approvals.

  1. Company Details: edit your profile, products/services, and add/rename/remove team roles.
  2. Currency & High-value threshold: set the amount above which payments/invoices are flagged “verify before approving”.
  3. Owner sign-off: turn on to route high-value items to the owner for approval; off lets finance handle them with a verify flag.
  4. Workspace ID: copy this for WhatsApp routing setup.

18 · Using the Mobile App

For Everyone

DecisionOS works right in your phone’s browser — no install needed. The layout adapts: a bottom tab bar for the main screens and a ☰ menu for everything else.

  • Bottom tabs: Desk, Brief, Work, People, Brain.
  • Swipe gestures: in the feed, swipe right to view, left to dismiss.
  • Tap-to-read: long execution-guide steps open in a readable popup.
  • Add to Home Screen: use your browser’s “Add to Home Screen” for an app-like icon.
