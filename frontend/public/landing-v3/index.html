The things founders ask first.

Straight answers. No sales call needed.

Is this just ChatGPT with extra steps?

No. ChatGPT answers questions and forgets between chats. DecisionOS remembers every decision permanently, turns talk into real tasks and records automatically, and runs full approval workflows, not just text replies.

What happens to a decision that needs my approval?

It lands in your queue with the original context attached. You edit, approve, or reassign it directly. Sensitive items are gated behind owner approval by default, so nothing sensitive executes without you.

Is my company's data safe?

Yes. DecisionOS is multi-tenant and isolated per company, with role-based access so people only see what they should, and secure sign-in throughout. Enterprise discipline, founder simplicity.

Do I have to change how my team already works?

No. DecisionOS listens on the channels your team already uses: voice, text, WhatsApp, PDFs, photos and spreadsheets. If your team can send a WhatsApp message, they can run DecisionOS.

How long before it actually understands my business?

From day one it captures and executes. Over time, every decision, approval and workflow becomes a data point about how your specific business runs. That is your Business DNA, and it keeps compounding the longer you run on it.

What's live today, and what is still coming?

Capture and Organize, which turn voice, text and WhatsApp into structured, executed work, are live today. Manage, Recommend, and a fully autonomous mode are the next stages on the roadmap. We will always tell you plainly which is which.

What if the AI gets a decision wrong?

Sensitive and high-impact items are gated behind owner approval before anything executes, and every action carries a full trail back to who decided what. Nothing runs silently. You can always see it, correct it, and override it.

Does this replace my accounting software or ERP?

DecisionOS runs the operating layer above your records: decisions, tasks, approvals, and a live finance view. Talk to us about your specific stack during onboarding and we will map out exactly how it fits alongside what you already use.
