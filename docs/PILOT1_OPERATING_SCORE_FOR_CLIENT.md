# How your Operating Score is worked out

*What it measures, what was wrong with it, and what we changed.*

---

## What the score is

Your Operating Score is one number out of 100 that sums up how smoothly the
business is running. It is built from four parts, and each part counts for a
share of the total:

| Part | What it looks at | Share of the total |
|---|---|---|
| **Execution** | Getting work done on time | 35% |
| **Finance** | Money coming in from customers | 25% |
| **Sales** (really: decisions) | Decisions raised and approved | 20% |
| **Responsiveness** | Closing loops — complaints and missed deadlines | 20% |

## How each part is worked out

**Execution.** Of all the work that is either finished or still open, how much
is finished. Then points come off for work that is past its deadline — up to
40 points if every open task is late.

**Finance.** Of everything you have billed customers, how much has been
received. Then 5 points come off for every customer invoice that is past its
due date and not fully paid.

**Sales.** Of the decisions raised, how many have been approved.

**Responsiveness.** Starts at 100. Twelve points come off for every open
customer complaint, and three points for every task past its deadline.

The overall score is the four parts combined in the shares above.

---

## What was wrong, and what we changed

### 1. Work due today was being counted as late — all day

A task due today was treated as overdue from the first minute of the day. So on
any day with work due, the score was pulled down for tasks that were not late
at all — in Execution, in Responsiveness, and in each person's own score.
Customer invoices due today were counted as overdue in the same way.

**Now:** a task is late only once its day is over. If it was given a time,
it is late once that time has passed. The day and the time are India's. This
is the same way your task list shows "Overdue", so the score and the list now
agree.

### 2. Parts you have not started using were given a made-up 70

If there were no invoices yet, Finance was scored 70. If no decisions had been
raised, Sales was scored 70. These were not measured — they were a default, and
they could make up almost half of the overall score for a business that had not
started using those parts yet.

**Now:** a part with nothing to measure yet is left out, and the score is
worked out from the parts you do use. On the Operating Score page it reads
*"Nothing to score yet — left out of the total"*.

> Your score may move after this change, and it may go down. That is because
> it no longer gives credit for things that have not happened yet. The new
> number is the honest one.

## A worked example

This is our demonstration company, *Shree Balaji Textiles*, at 2:25 pm on
21 September.

**The business that day**

- 19 tasks: 1 finished, 18 still open. Of the open ones, 5 are past their
  deadline and 3 more are **due today**.
- Customers have been billed ₹25,89,000, and ₹20,15,000 has been received.
  Two invoices are past their due date and not fully paid.
- 6 decisions raised; none approved yet.
- 1 open customer complaint (a second one has been resolved).

**Before the change**

The 3 tasks due today were counted as late, so the score saw **8** late tasks.

| Part | How it came out | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Execution | 1 of 19 finished is about 5 points; 8 of the 18 open late takes off about 18 → below zero, shown as | **0** |
| Finance | 77.8% received, minus 2 late invoices × 5 | **68** |
| Sales | 0 of 6 approved | **0** |
| Responsiveness | 100 − 12 (1 complaint) − 24 (8 late tasks × 3) | **64** |
| **Overall** | (0 × 35% + 68 × 25% + 0 × 20% + 64 × 20%) | **30** |

**After the change**

Only the 5 tasks whose day has actually passed are late.

| Part | How it comes out | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Execution | about 5 points, minus about 11 (5 of 18 open late) → below zero, shown as | **0** |
| Finance | unchanged | **68** |
| Sales | unchanged | **0** |
| Responsiveness | 100 − 12 − 15 (5 late tasks × 3) | **73** |
| **Overall** | (0 × 35% + 68 × 25% + 0 × 20% + 73 × 20%) | **32** |

**The same company in its first weeks**

Now say the company is using tasks only — no invoices, no decisions and no
complaints yet.

- **Before:** Finance and Sales were each given the made-up 70. With 8 tasks
  counted late, Responsiveness was 100 − 24 = 76. Overall:
  (0 × 35% + 70 × 25% + 70 × 20% + 76 × 20%) = **47**.
- **After:** Finance and Sales are left out. Responsiveness is 100 − 15 = 85.
  The overall is worked out from Execution and Responsiveness only, in their
  own shares: (0 × 35 + 85 × 20) ÷ 55 = **31**.

The first 47 looked healthier, but 31.5 of those points were for parts of the
business that had not been used yet.
